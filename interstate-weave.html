How to play

Drive fast, pass traffic within a metre for close calls, and chain them for big combos. Faster driving multiplies every point. Brushing a car only damages you. A real hit ends the run.

Modes

  • Levels100 runs to a named exit, with time limits and close-call quotas.
  • EndlessNo finish line. Traffic builds the farther you go.
  • PursuitPolice box you in. Escape to raise the heat.
  • Rush hourBumper-to-bumper traffic. Cut up and thread gaps to keep your flow multiplier high.

Tips

Earn career points in any mode to unlock four faster cars in the garage.

Pick Easy, Normal or Hard on the menu. Try Two-way roads for double points on the wrong side.

Use the restart button next to the settings gear to start the run over.
